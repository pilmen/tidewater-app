Privacy Policy

Tidewater Photo Editor for iPad and Mac · Effective October 20, 2026

Tidewater is a photo editor that works on files you keep on your own device. The developer does not collect, store or sell any information about you or your photos.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Tidewater has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server run by the developer. Crash reports reach the developer only if you choose to share them with app developers in your device's settings, and Apple delivers them without identifying you.

Your photos

Photos stay on your device and in the folders you choose. Your edits are saved as small sidecar files next to the originals (.tidewater.json and, for compatibility with other apps, .xmp). If those folders are in iCloud Drive or another file-syncing service, that service syncs them under its own privacy policy.

When you open photos from your Photos library, Tidewater asks for permission and reads only the photos you open in the app. Exports are written only where you choose.

The AI Assistant (optional)

The AI Assistant is off until you set it up with an API key from an AI provider you choose, such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Meta or OpenRouter. You can also point it at a model running on your own computer.

When you ask the Assistant to edit a photo, Tidewater sends these directly from your device to that provider:

  • your request, as you typed it
  • a downsized JPEG preview of the photo you're editing (at most 1024 pixels on the long side)
  • basic image statistics, such as a brightness histogram and which subjects, people or sky were detected on your device
  • the edit settings the Assistant reads or changes

The full-size original is never uploaded. Nothing passes through the developer. The provider handles this data under the terms and privacy policy of your account with them. Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain and is sent only to that provider.

Children

Tidewater is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
