Support

Found a bug, have a camera whose RAW files won't open, or want to suggest a feature? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your device, its system version, and the camera model if the problem is with a RAW file.

Common questions

Which RAW formats are supported?

Tidewater uses Apple's RAW engine, so it opens the cameras your iPad or Mac supports. Apple publishes the list on its support site; search for "supported RAW formats" for iPadOS or macOS. JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and PNG files open too.

Where are my edits saved?

Next to each photo, in a small .tidewater.json file. Your originals are never changed. Move or sync the folder and the edits come with it. Ratings, flags, keywords and crop are also written to an .xmp file that other photo apps can read.

Do I need to buy it separately for iPad and Mac?

No. One purchase covers both.

How do I set up the AI Assistant?

Open the Assistant panel, choose a provider, and paste an API key from that provider's website. You pay the provider directly for what you use. The Assistant only adjusts settings you can see and change yourself, and nothing is applied until you accept it.

What does the AI Assistant send?

A downsized preview of the photo you're editing plus your request, straight to the provider you chose. See the Privacy Policy for details.
